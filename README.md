# MUNSOON

### AI-Powered Energy Resilience for Monsoon-Prone Solar Homes

> **"Will my house make it through tonight?"**

---

## 🌧️ Where MUNSOON Started

The idea for MUNSOON came from a simple observation at home in Kerala.

We had rooftop solar, and on normal days, the system could generate more electricity than the house needed. But during the monsoon, everything changed.

The sky would stay cloudy for hours. Solar generation would drop. And when heavy rain and strong winds caused a grid outage, the problem became bigger: **there was less solar energy exactly when reliable electricity was needed most.**

It raised a simple question:

> **Can we predict an energy shortage before it happens, instead of reacting after the power goes out?**

That question became **MUNSOON**.

---

## 💡 The Idea

MUNSOON is an AI-powered prototype that helps solar-powered households prepare for periods of low solar generation and possible grid disruptions.

Instead of simply showing how much energy is available **right now**, MUNSOON looks ahead and asks:

**How much energy might I have?  
How much might I need?  
Will my battery last?  
What should I do before the shortage happens?**

The goal is to turn weather uncertainty into **proactive energy decisions**.
